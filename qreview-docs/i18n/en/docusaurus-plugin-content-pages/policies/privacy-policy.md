# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 14, 2025**

This Privacy Policy describes how QArify ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information through the QArify service ("Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## **1. Information We Collect**

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials, for example, through third-party services like Google Sign-In.
- **Cloud-Synchronized Data:** Only if you choose to use our cloud features, we collect and process your project data, which may include test cases, test suites, project configuration files, and test results. We do not collect data that you use only locally on your computer.
- **Usage Data:** To improve the Service, we collect anonymized data about how you interact with our CLI, Local UI, and web platform. This data includes information such as feature usage frequency, performance metrics, and error logs, and cannot be used to personally identify you.
- **Payment Information:** If you subscribe to a paid plan, our trusted third-party payment processor will collect and handle your payment information. We do not directly store sensitive financial information, such as your full credit card number.
- **Automatically Collected Information:** When you access the Service, we may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and operating system.

## **2. How We Use Information**

- **To Provide and Maintain the Service:** We use your information to deliver the core functionalities of the Service, maintain your account, and troubleshoot issues.
- **For Service Improvement, Research, and Development:** We use collected data (particularly anonymized project and usage data) for statistical analysis and to train our machine learning models. This helps us enhance our AI-powered features, such as test generation and analysis, based on the license granted in our Terms of Service (Section 6: Intellectual Property Rights).
- **To Communicate with Users:** We use your email address to send you important information about the Service, including updates, security alerts, and support-related messages.
- **To Process Payments:** We use your information to process billing for our paid plans.
- **To Enforce Our Terms:** We may use information to prevent misuse of the Service and to enforce our Terms of Service, including the provisions in Section 4 (User Responsibilities and Prohibited Use).

## **3. Data Storage, Security, and Transfer**

- **Data Storage and Processing:** Your information is stored and processed on the servers of reputable third-party cloud service providers (e.g., Google Cloud Platform, Amazon Web Services), which may be located in various countries, including the Republic of Korea. Using global data centers helps us improve the speed and reliability of the Service.
- **Data Security:** We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include data encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
- **Data Retention:** We retain your personal information as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personally identifiable information will be permanently deleted from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal obligations (e.g., tax records) or for resolving disputes. Anonymized data may be kept for longer periods for statistical and service improvement purposes.

## **4. Data Sharing and Disclosure**

- We do not sell your personal data or provide it to third parties for their marketing purposes.
- **Service Providers:** We may share information with trusted third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as cloud infrastructure hosting, payment processing, and email delivery. These providers are given access only to the information necessary to perform their functions.

## **5. Your Rights**

- **Access and Correction:** You can access and update your personal information at any time through the web dashboard.
- **Deletion and Withdrawal of Consent:** You may request the deletion of your personal information and withdraw your consent to our data processing by deleting your account through the web dashboard.
- **Other Rights:** Depending on applicable laws, you may have other rights, such as the right to request a copy of your data (data portability) or to restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.
